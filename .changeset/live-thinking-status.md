**Long reasoning shows a live status line.** At high thinking levels a thread used to show only the typing indicator for 25–70 s before the first output. When a reasoning block runs longer than 4 s, Kimaki now posts `-# thinking · 12s · <latest summary heading>`, edits it at most every 5 s, and finalizes it as `-# thought for 31s · <heading>` before the answer. Short blocks post nothing.
